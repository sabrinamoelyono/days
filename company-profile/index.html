01Who we are

PT Vakansi Media Global is the company behind DAYS, a travel concierge for people who travel because of something: a festival lineup, a race weekend, the urge to go somewhere wild.

We don't sell trips off a shelf. Every DAYS package is built around one moment, like Glastonbury in June, a Grand Prix weekend or a week in the Bornean rainforest. We handle the logistics so you can be fully there when it happens.

We're based in Tebet, South Jakarta. Our travellers are Indonesian, and we build for Southeast Asian routes, time zones and cultural references. The company is licensed as a travel agency (biro perjalanan wisata) and a tourism information provider, with management consulting in its registered scope.

07Brand identity

How the DAYS brand looks and sounds.

Positioning

DAYS is a travel concierge for people who follow culture. The audience is 25 to 45, Jakarta-based and well-travelled, and follows music, sport and design closely. They've moved on from group tours.

What we offer

Music festivals (Glastonbury, Primavera Sound, Dekmantel), motorsport and major sport (F1 Grand Prix weekends, the Australian Open), nature trips such as Into The Wild: Borneo, and culture and city trips. Each package has its own name and is built around one event.

Voice

Cool and editorial. Confident. Specific over generic. Upscale but easy to approach, like a well-travelled friend who always knows the right place.

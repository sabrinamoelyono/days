## Who we are

PT Vakansi Media Global is the company behind **DAYS**, a travel concierge for people who travel *because of* something: a festival lineup, a race weekend, the urge to go somewhere wild.

Every DAYS package is built around one moment, like Glastonbury in June, a Grand Prix weekend or a week in the Bornean rainforest. We handle the logistics so you can be fully there when it happens.

We're based in Tebet, South Jakarta. Our travellers are Indonesian, and we build for Southeast Asian routes, time zones and cultural references.

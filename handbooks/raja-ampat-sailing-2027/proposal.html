Be there.
Be part of it.

Saksikan aksinya. Rasakan serunya.

Some places are best seen from the water. Raja Ampat is one of them.

Together with Amaya Explorer Indonesia – Phinisi Archipelago, we’ve planned eight days aboard a traditional phinisi through the centre of Raja Ampat: around twenty dives across Piaynemo, Gam, the Dampier Strait and Mansuar, with the boat as your base between them.

It’s a chance to slow down, switch off, and see Indonesia from a different angle.

Bersama Amaya Explorer Indonesia – Phinisi Archipelago, kami merancang delapan hari di atas kapal phinisi tradisional melintasi Raja Ampat bagian tengah: sekitar dua puluh kali penyelaman di Piaynemo, Gam, Selat Dampier, dan Mansuar.

Beyond the destination, it’s about the journey.

The boat · Kapal

Amaya Explorer

A 35-metre phinisi, built by hand in Bulukumba, South Sulawesi, by craftsmen carrying on generations of boatbuilding. Two years in the making.

The crew comes from across Indonesia, experienced on the water and ready for whatever the day needs. Diving leads, but it isn’t the only way to spend the day: stand-up paddling, kayaking, snorkelling, freediving and yoga are all on board.

Phinisi sepanjang 35 meter, dibuat tangan oleh pengrajin di Bulukumba, Sulawesi Selatan. Selain menyelam, tersedia paddle board, kayak, snorkeling, freediving, dan yoga.

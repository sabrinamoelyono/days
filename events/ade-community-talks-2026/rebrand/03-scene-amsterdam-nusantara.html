Nusantara Elsewhere connects Indonesia’s electronic music scene with the world. This edition asks how we turn inter­national interest in Indonesia into a way out for our own: getting Indonesian artists recognized, booked and heard in Amsterdam and the wider European festival scene.
From media and labels to the people who built the scene, all in one room to talk about growing at home and getting widely recog­nized.
This afternoon will be a start, a regular meeting point, and a path for more Indonesian artists to reach stages abroad.

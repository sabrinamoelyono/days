Jakarta → Amsterdam
They fly in.
Ours fly out.
Global DJs are keen to play Asia, and Jakarta's promoters bring them in all year round.
Getting Recognized asks how we turn that into a way out for our own: getting Indonesian artists noticed, booked and heard abroad, starting with Amsterdam.
This afternoon is a start. Over the next few years, we want it to become a regular meeting point, and a path for more Indonesian artists to reach stages in Amsterdam and beyond.

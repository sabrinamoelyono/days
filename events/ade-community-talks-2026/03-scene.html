Indonesia's electronic music scene has grown fast, but the people behind it rarely get the chance to sit down together.
That's why we're doing this: to build a stronger community, to put our own talent in the spotlight, and to connect them with audiences beyond Indonesia.
This afternoon is a start. Over the next few years, we want it to become a regular meeting point, and a way for more Indonesian artists to reach stages around the world.
Come for the talk.
Stay for the people.

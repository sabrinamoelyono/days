ADE Community Talks bridges Indonesia's electronic music scene with Amsterdam Dance Event.
We're here to build a stronger community at home, put our own talent in the spotlight, and connect them with the global scene.
This afternoon is a start. Over the next few years, we want it to become a regular meeting point, and a path for more Indonesian artists to reach ADE in Amsterdam and stages around the world.

Panel briefing
Marc Lankreijer
Host & Moderator | Netherlands
Founder of AMS Bookings (1999), a boutique agency for DJs and live acts with a special focus on Asia. Marc has built an extensive network connecting Asian and European music professionals, has contributed to ADE and initiated Asia-focused conferences.
Nakadia
Thailand / Berlin
Pioneering Thai techno DJ, producer and promoter, touring internationally since 2003. Her career includes releases on Carl Cox’s Intec, Tomorrowland Belgium, Carl Cox at UNVRS Ibiza in 2026 and the forthcoming Tomorrowland Thailand, with 2,500+ shows in more than 100 countries.
Arsit Prachaseri
Thailand
Founder of Retox Sessions, Together Festival and 808 Festival. An experienced promoter across major international festivals, club bookings and new audiences, he brings the view of a business balancing international headliners, commercial risk and opportunities for local talent.
Daniel “DKAY” Kim
South Korea
Talent Relations Director at UNDERCITY, the Seoul venue opened in May 2025 and run by seven co-founders. His bookings include Ben Klock, Nicole Moudaber, Bart Skils and Brutalismus 3000. He develops audiences across techno styles and connects international agents with Asian venues and promoters.
BETTER / Sunny Kim
South Korea
BETTER is the artist name of Sunny Kim, a Seoul-based DJ and producer blending house and tech house with underground influences. Alongside performances across Asia and Europe, she brings around ten years of venue promotion and current DJ-booking work, connecting artist, promoter and local audience.
Sabrina Moelyono
Indonesia
Founder of DAYS, connecting Indonesian music travellers with European club and festival culture, and Community Manager at Tripo 3000. Working across Jakarta, Bali and international networks, she links audiences, artists and partners, with a focus on grassroots communities and lasting cultural exchange.

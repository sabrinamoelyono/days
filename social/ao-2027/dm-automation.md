# AO27 — Instagram comment-to-DM automation

**Keyword:** `CLOSER` (also trigger on `closer`, `AO27`, `info`, `price`, `harga`)
Ties back to the campaign line, is easy to type, and won't fire on ordinary comments.

**Package details are not final.** Everything in `[brackets]` is filled in once the packages are confirmed. Keep the structure; only swap the brackets. Send this copy to AO Travel & Experiences for approval with the rest (Hightail, Word).

---

## 1. Call to action

Add as the last line of each caption, and as text on the last Story (or a sticker in the empty lower area, never over a photo).

**Post caption (EN):** Comment CLOSER and we'll send you the AO27 package details.
**Post caption (ID):** Komen CLOSER, kami kirim detail paket AO27 lewat DM.

**Story:** Reply CLOSER for the package details. / Balas CLOSER untuk detail paket.

---

## 2. Public reply to the comment

Rotate these so Instagram doesn't read the replies as spam. Never put prices in public replies.

1. Sent to your DMs. See you closer to the court.
2. Check your inbox. Your AO27 starts there.
3. Just sent it over. Have a look.
4. Sudah kami kirim ke DM kamu.
5. Cek DM ya, detailnya sudah kami kirim.

---

## 3. DM flow

### DM 1: Opener (sent automatically, with a button)
Instagram requires the first automated DM to get a tap before you send links or longer details.

> Hi {first_name}, thanks for commenting.
>
> DAYS is an AO Travel & Experiences official reseller for the Australian Open 2027. Every package comes with a lower level seat at Rod Laver Arena, close to the court.
>
> Want the details?
>
> Hai {first_name}, terima kasih sudah komen. Mau lihat detail paketnya?

**Button:** `Show me the packages` / `Lihat paket`

### DM 2: The four rounds (after the tap)

> Australian Open 2027. Choose your round:
>
> **Quarterfinals** · [dates] · from IDR [price]
> **Semifinals** · [dates] · from IDR [price]
> **Finals** · [dates] · from IDR [price]
> **Semifinal + Final** · [dates] · from IDR [price]
>
> Every package includes: [lower level seat at Rod Laver Arena · AO Reserve access · hotel nights · flights, if included]
>
> Which round are you looking at?

**Buttons:** `Quarterfinals` · `Semifinals` · `Finals` · `Semifinal + Final` · `Not sure yet`

### DM 3: Round details (one message per button)

> **[ROUND NAME]** · [dates]
> [Matches: e.g. two night sessions at Rod Laver Arena]
> [Seat: lower level, close to the court]
> [Hospitality: AO Reserve — The Gallery]
> [Stay: x days, x nights at hotel]
> [Flights: included / not included]
> IDR [price] / person
>
> Seats are limited. Want us to hold a spot while we talk it through?

**Buttons:** `Yes, contact me` · `Ask a question`

**"Not sure yet":**
> No problem. Tell us when you can travel and who's coming, and we'll suggest the round that fits. What dates work for you?
> → hand over to the team.

### DM 4: Lead capture (after "Yes, contact me")

> Great. Three quick things so the team can prepare:
> 1. Your name
> 2. How many travellers
> 3. Your WhatsApp number
>
> Nama, jumlah peserta, dan nomor WhatsApp kamu, ya.

### DM 5: Handover

> Thank you, {first_name}. Someone from DAYS will reach out on WhatsApp within [1 business day]. If you'd rather talk now: +62 812-2000-0185.
>
> Terima kasih! Tim DAYS akan menghubungi kamu lewat WhatsApp dalam [1 hari kerja].

**Tag the lead** with: round chosen · travellers · WhatsApp · date. Turn off the automation for that conversation once a person replies, so the bot doesn't talk over the team.

### Out of hours (optional)

> Thanks for your message. Our team is offline now and will reply from [09:00 WIB]. Package details are above in the meantime.

---

## 4. Rules to keep

- Write "AO Travel & Experiences official reseller" exactly; always "&", never "and".
- "Quarterfinals" and "Semifinals" are one word.
- No prices in public comments; prices only in DMs.
- Don't promise availability ("guaranteed seat") until the package is confirmed.
- Update every `[bracket]` the moment the package changes, and keep the DM, captions and slides in step.

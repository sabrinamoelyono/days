Sabrina
Moelyono

Founder, DAYS ✦ Community Manager, Tripo 3000

Sabrina Moelyono works where music, travel and community meet. As founder of DAYS, a Jakarta-based travel media company, Sabrina connects Indonesia's music lovers with Europe's festivals and club culture: telling the stories of those scenes, then taking people there.

As community manager at Tripo 3000, the multi-award-winning partymaker behind Asia KolektifA and Space Trip, Sabrina looks after the crowd, artists and partners that turn a series of parties into a scene.

Between the two, Sabrina sees both sides of the dancefloor: how a local scene is built from the ground up, and how a new generation of Southeast Asian fans crosses continents for the music they love.

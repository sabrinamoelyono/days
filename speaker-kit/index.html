What we do

Travel media

Editorial and social content on Europe's festivals, clubs and city scenes, written for Indonesian readers: who's playing, why it matters, how to get there from Jakarta.

Curated music travel

Named, end-to-end trips built around one moment: Dekmantel in August, Primavera in May, Glastonbury in June, ADE in October. We handle the logistics so our travellers can be fully present.

Global connector

Partnerships, introductions and market entry between Indonesian and European promoters, festivals, venues, artists and brands.

Why it matters to the music industry

Southeast Asia is one of the fastest-growing audiences for electronic music, and Indonesia, with 280 million people and one of the youngest populations in the region, is its biggest. A new generation of Indonesian fans already follows European lineups in real time. They don't need persuading; they need a trusted route in.

For European festivals and artists, DAYS is a way to reach that audience with context and credibility rather than paid ads. For Indonesian fans and scenes, we're the friend who has already been, knows the right stage, and makes the introduction.

We sell trust before we sell trips.

Bio

Sabrina works where music, travel and community meet, as the founder of DAYS, a Jakarta-based travel media company that connects Indonesia's music lovers with Europe's festivals and club culture, telling the stories of those scenes and then taking people there.

Sabrina is also community manager at Tripo, the award-winning partymaker, looking after the crowd, artists and partners that turn a series of parties into a scene.

Between the two, Sabrina has a rare view of both sides of the dancefloor: how a local scene is built from the ground up, and how a new generation of Southeast Asian fans is crossing continents for the music they love. Through DAYS, the goal is to make the Indonesia–Europe link a two-way street for artists, promoters and audiences.

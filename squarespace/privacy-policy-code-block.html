02Data we collect

We only collect what we need to plan, book and run your trip, and to keep in touch if you ask us to.

Data you give us

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, city of residence.
  • Enquiry details: the package or event you're interested in, travel dates, group size, budget range and preferences you share in a form, DM or chat.
  • Booking and travel documents: full legal name, date of birth, nationality, passport number and expiry, ID card (KTP) details, visa information and copies of documents needed to book flights, accommodation, event tickets or visas.
  • Special requirements: dietary needs, accessibility needs, or health information you choose to tell us so we can arrange your trip safely. We treat health information as specific personal data under UU PDP and only use it with your explicit consent.
  • Emergency contact: the name and phone number of someone we can reach if something goes wrong while you travel. Please tell them you've shared their details with us.
  • Payment information: payment confirmations, bank transfer receipts and invoicing details. Card payments are processed by our payment providers; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Communications: messages, emails, voice notes and feedback you send us.

Data we collect automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages viewed, referring page and time spent on our website.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see Cookies and the Meta Pixel.

Data from third parties

  • Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp): see the next section.
  • Travel partners: booking references, confirmations and itinerary changes from airlines, hotels, ticketing platforms and local operators.
  • A person booking for you: if a friend or family member books a group trip, they may give us your name and travel details. They should tell you about this policy.

  • Instagram and Facebook Messenger messages: your public profile name, username, profile picture and the content of messages you send to our accounts.

We use this data only to reply to you, handle your enquiry or booking, and measure and improve our advertising, as described in this policy.

Our commitments on Meta Platform Data. We do not sell, license or purchase data received from Meta. We do not use it to discriminate, to make decisions about eligibility for housing, employment, insurance, credit or similar, for surveillance, or to build profiles of individuals beyond what you'd expect from your conversation with us. We do not share it with data brokers. We only keep it for as long as we need it for the purposes described here, and we delete it when you ask us to (see Deleting your data).

Meta processes your data under its own policies. To learn more, read the Meta Privacy Policy and the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

04How we use your data

  • To answer your enquiries and send you trip proposals and quotes.
  • To book and run your trip: reserving flights, accommodation, event tickets and transfers, applying for visas, sending itineraries and supporting you on the ground.
  • To take payment, issue invoices and handle refunds.
  • To keep you safe while travelling, including contacting your emergency contact if needed.
  • To send updates you asked for, such as new package launches and early-access drops. You can opt out at any time by replying "STOP", clicking unsubscribe, or messaging us.
  • To advertise and measure our campaigns on Meta and other platforms, including showing DAYS ads to people who visited our website or messaged us, and to people with similar interests.
  • To improve our website and services using aggregated analytics.
  • To meet legal obligations, including tax, accounting, tourism licensing and requests from authorities where the law requires it.
  • To prevent fraud and protect our business, customers and partners.

We do not use automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

05Legal basis

Under UU PDP (Article 20), we process personal data on one or more of these grounds:

  • Contract: to take steps you ask for before booking and to deliver the trip you booked.
  • Consent: for marketing messages, non-essential cookies and the Meta Pixel, and any health or other specific personal data. You can withdraw consent at any time; this doesn't affect processing that happened before.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with Indonesian tax, tourism and other laws.
  • Vital interests: to protect your life or health in an emergency.
  • Legitimate interests: to run and improve our business, secure our systems and prevent fraud, balanced against your rights.

06Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where needed for the purposes above. Travel documents have stricter rules, set out in Your travel documents.

  • Travel suppliers: airlines, hotels and other accommodation, event and festival organisers, ticketing platforms, transfer companies, local guides and tour operators who deliver parts of your trip.
  • Visa and immigration bodies, embassies and visa application centres, when you ask us to help with a visa.
  • Insurance providers, if you buy travel insurance through us.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf: payment processors and banks, website hosting, email and messaging tools (including Meta and Google), cloud storage, analytics and accounting software. They may only use your data to provide services to us.
  • Advertising partners, including Meta, for ad delivery and measurement (see Cookies and the Meta Pixel).
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, auditors and tax consultants.
  • Authorities, when required by law, court order or to protect someone's safety.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is merged or sold. We'll tell you before your data falls under a different privacy policy.

07Your travel documents

To book your trip, you'll often send us documents like your passport, KTP, visa, bank statements, employment letters or photos. We treat everything you share as confidential information that stays yours. Sharing it with us gives us no rights over it beyond what we need to deliver your trip.

We only share documents where your trip needs them

We use and share your documents only for the service you asked for, and only with the people who need them to deliver it:

  • Airlines and booking systems, to issue your flight tickets.
  • VFS Global, TLScontact, BLS International, embassies and consulates, to submit your visa application.
  • Hotels and other accommodation, for check-in and registration where local law requires it.
  • Event and festival organisers and ticketing platforms, where tickets are issued in your name or need ID.
  • Transfer companies, local guides and tour operators running parts of your trip.
  • Travel insurers, if you buy insurance through us.
  • DAYS staff, agents and professional advisers who need the documents to deliver your trip.

Each of them gets only the documents and details needed for their part of the trip. For example, an event organiser gets your name, not a copy of your bank statement. Everyone at DAYS who handles your documents, including our agents and advisers, must keep them confidential. These commitments apply from the moment you send us a document, and continue after your trip ends until the document is deleted.

What we don't do

  • We never use your documents for marketing, advertising or anything unrelated to your booking.
  • We never sell them or share them with Meta or other advertising partners.
  • We don't copy or reproduce them except when we need to send them to one of the parties above for your trip.

When we delete them

We delete your passport, ID, visa documents and other supporting documents within 90 days after your trip ends. If your trip is cancelled or doesn't go ahead, we delete them within 90 days of the cancellation. We delete them from email, chat, cloud storage and our devices, and tell any staff or agents who received copies to do the same.

You can ask us to return or delete your documents sooner at any time (see Deleting your data). We'll only keep something longer if a law, court process or ongoing dispute requires it, and we'll tell you if that happens. Once a document has gone to an airline, embassy or visa centre, it's handled under that organisation's own privacy policy.

If the law requires us to disclose them

If a court, government agency or regulator legally requires us to hand over your information, we'll tell you in writing before we disclose it, unless the law doesn't allow us to.

08International transfers

Most DAYS trips go abroad, so we have to send some of your data to suppliers outside Indonesia, for example to a hotel in the United Kingdom or a festival organiser in Spain. Some of our service providers (such as Meta and Google) also store data outside Indonesia.

When we transfer data abroad, we follow Article 56 of UU PDP: we transfer it to countries with an equivalent or higher level of protection, or use appropriate safeguards such as contractual commitments, or rely on your consent where no other safeguard is available. Transfers needed to book the trip you ask for are part of delivering that contract.

09Cookies and the Meta Pixel

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies (pixels, local storage, SDKs).

  • Essential cookies keep the website working and secure. They can't be switched off.
  • Analytics cookies help us understand which pages people use, in aggregate.
  • Advertising cookies, including the Meta Pixel, help us measure and improve our ads.

Meta Pixel and Conversions API

We use the Meta Pixel on our website and may use Meta's Conversions API to send Meta information about actions you take on our website, such as pages viewed, enquiry forms submitted and bookings made. This may include your IP address, browser information, a cookie identifier and, for the Conversions API, hashed (scrambled) contact details such as your email or phone number.

Third parties, including Meta, may use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies to collect or receive information from our website and elsewhere on the internet and use that information to provide measurement services and target ads. Meta matches this information with its users to deliver and measure DAYS ads and to improve its own advertising products, under the Meta Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Manage or decline non-essential cookies using our cookie banner, or block them in your browser settings.
  • Control how Meta uses data from other websites and apps to show you ads in your Meta Ad Preferences, and disconnect past activity under Your activity off Meta technologies.
  • Opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies at aboutads.info/choices (US) or youronlinechoices.eu (EU).
  • On mobile devices, use your device's ad settings to limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID.

11Your rights

Under UU PDP, and other data protection laws where they apply to you, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about who is processing your data and why (this policy).
  • Access your personal data and get a copy of it.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your personal data, ask us to return documents you sent us, or ask us to stop processing it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including for marketing and the Meta Pixel.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to automated decision-making.
  • Restrict or delay processing in certain cases.
  • Data portability: receive your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format, or have it sent to another controller.
  • Claim compensation for violations of your data rights, as provided by law.

To use any of these rights, email lets@talkaboutdays.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We'll respond within 3 × 24 hours for access, correction and deletion requests as required by UU PDP, and tell you if we need more time or can't fully meet a request (for example, where we must keep invoices for tax law).

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please talk to us first. You can also complain to the Indonesian personal data protection authority or the Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs (Komdigi).

12Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete all personal data we hold about you at any time, including data we received through Facebook, Instagram, Messenger or WhatsApp.

How to request deletion

  1. Email lets@talkaboutdays.com with the subject line "Data deletion request", or send us a WhatsApp message on +62 812-2000-0185, or a DM on Instagram saying "Delete my data".
  2. Include your full name and the email address, phone number or Instagram / Facebook username you used with us, so we can find your records.
  3. We'll confirm we received your request and may ask you to verify your identity.
  4. We'll delete your data from our systems within 3 × 24 hours of verifying your request, and send you a confirmation with a reference number. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

If you used Facebook or Instagram Login

You can also remove DAYS's access in your Meta account: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites on Facebook (or Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites on Instagram), find DAYS and select Remove. Then send us a deletion request using the steps above to delete the data we already received.

What we may have to keep

We may keep a limited amount of data where the law requires it, such as invoices and payment records for tax purposes, or where needed to handle an ongoing claim. We'll tell you what we're keeping and why, and delete it when the obligation ends.

You can also delete messages from your own side of a conversation in Instagram, Messenger or WhatsApp, but that won't remove copies we hold. Send us a request to remove ours.

Step-by-step instructions are also on our data deletion page.

13Security

We protect your data with reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access limited to staff who need it, two-factor authentication on our business accounts, and secure storage for travel documents. No system is completely secure, so please share passport and ID copies only through the channels we ask you to use.

If a data breach is likely to affect you, we'll notify you and the relevant authority in writing within 3 × 24 hours of becoming aware of it, as required by Article 46 of UU PDP, explaining what happened, what data was involved and what we're doing about it.

14Children

Our website and services are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. When a child travels on a DAYS trip, we collect their data only from a parent or legal guardian, with their consent. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.
